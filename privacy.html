YEETATO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

YEETATO is a potato-launching game for Android, published by Koossie. This page explains what the game does with your data.

Data we collect

We (the developer) collect nothing: YEETATO has no accounts, no purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own, and your game progress never leaves your phone.

Advertising

YEETATO shows ads from Google AdMob and Unity Ads: ads you choose to watch for a reward (for example triple spuds, keep flying, double a chore or level-up reward, free spuds), and an occasional full-screen ad between runs. To serve and measure ads, these networks receive data from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. They process it under their own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Unity Ads privacy policy

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ads. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). The game itself plays fine offline; without a connection there are simply no ads.

Updates

YEETATO uses Google's Play In-App Updates library to ask the Google Play Store app on your phone whether a newer version exists, and to install it. That conversation happens between the game and the Play Store on your device; YEETATO itself sends nothing to us. The Play Store's own handling of data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (distances, spuds, upgrades, hats, achievements, settings and the leaderboard name you choose) is saved locally on your phone so you can pick up where you left off. It is never uploaded. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Delete everything, or by uninstalling the app. If Android's own device backup is switched on, the operating system may include this save in your personal Google backup; that is controlled by your phone's settings, not by us.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state so ads can load.
  • Advertising ID used by the ad networks above.
  • Vibration for haptic feedback when the potato hits things. It can be turned off in Settings.
  • Notifications for a single quiet notification when a new version of the game is ready. You can switch it off in Settings → Update notifications, or in Android's settings.
  • Run at startup so Android keeps the twice-daily update check scheduled after your phone restarts.

Children

YEETATO is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.
