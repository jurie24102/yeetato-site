YEETATO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

YEETATO is an offline potato-launching game for Android, published by Koossie. This page explains what the game does with your data. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

Data we collect

None. YEETATO has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (distances, spuds, upgrades, hats, achievements, settings and the leaderboard name you choose) is saved locally on your phone so you can pick up where you left off. It is never uploaded. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Delete everything, or by uninstalling the app. If Android's own device backup is switched on, the operating system may include this save in your personal Google backup; that is controlled by your phone's settings, not by us.

Permissions

  • Vibration — for haptic feedback when the potato hits things. It can be turned off in Settings.

Children

YEETATO collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.
